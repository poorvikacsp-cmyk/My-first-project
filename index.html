From survey to action

A practical four-step method

GREENSCAPE turns field observations into a realistic plan that can be piloted, monitored and improved over time.

01

Survey the space

Record location, dimensions, sunlight, existing trees, soil, drainage, access and community needs.

02

Understand conditions

Check local weather and use tested soil pH and moisture values where available.

03

Match the solution

Select suitable planting types, native species and water-conscious design options.

04

Care and measure

Assign maintenance ownership and track plant survival, water use, shade and feedback.

The GREENSCAPE promise

Right place. Right plant. Right care.

This is an educational planning prototype for urban greening. Final plant choices must be checked with local horticulture experts, municipal guidance and site-specific assessment. No automatic soil sensing or guaranteed environmental outcomes are claimed.
